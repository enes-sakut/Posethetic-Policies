Support

Contact Posethetic

Open a support request on GitHub. Include what you were trying to do and, if handy, the app version and iOS version. Requests are public. Leave out your email address, photos, receipts, account identifiers and any other private information.

For account or privacy questions, briefly describe the type of help you need and ask for a private follow-up.

Using the camera

Open Camera for a regular photo or choose a pose and tap Try this pose. Camera Controls lets you change guide options and adjust the timer. To review camera or Photos permission, open iPhone Settings from the app’s settings.

Save or delete a photo

After capture, tap Save to Photos to add the original shot to your Photos library. To delete a local capture, open Saved and use the photo’s delete action. Manage any separate copy saved to Photos in the Photos app.

Membership and account help

If Restore Purchases is shown in Settings, use it with the Apple Account you purchased with. Manage renewals separately in your Apple subscription settings.

If you have a Posethetic cloud account, open Settings → Manage cloud account → Delete cloud account when available. The confirmation shows what will be removed. For information on diagnostics and other data choices, see the Privacy Policy.
