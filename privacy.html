Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

This notice describes how Posethetic handles information in the iPhone app and on this support site. If you have a privacy question, use the contact option on the Support page.

Camera, pose guides and saved photos

You can browse the installed catalog and use the regular camera without an account. Browse history, favorites, style preferences, shot history and downloaded artwork are stored on your iPhone. When a catalog connection is available, the app requests pose information and artwork from Supabase. A refresh does not send your captures or camera frames.

Camera guidance analyzes supported live frames on your iPhone using Apple Vision. Posethetic opens the system Photos or sharing controls when you choose to save or share a capture. Once you share a photo, the destination you selected handles that copy.

Membership and accounts

Apple handles App Store payments. When membership services are configured, they use product and transaction information and a RevenueCat customer identifier to verify and restore access. RevenueCat uses an app-generated identifier for guests. If you link a Posethetic cloud account, the app uses its Supabase account identifier to connect membership with that account. Paid catalog requests use account and membership information to check access to protected artwork.

When cloud accounts are available, Sign in with Apple and Supabase help authenticate and manage your account. Apple Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Privacy Policy

AI Studio

Public cloud photo generation is currently unavailable. AI Studio can show example results in the app. If cloud generation becomes available, Posethetic will present the photo handling and provider information before asking for permission to upload a selected input photo.

Optional usage and crash diagnostics

On builds where Firebase is configured, you can allow usage and crash diagnostics in Settings. Posethetic’s optional custom analytics events use event names and do not include photos, pose landmarks, private links, tokens or account identifiers. When enabled, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process app or device identifiers, general device details and crash information. You can turn off collection for future events from Settings. Privacy and security in Firebase

Retention and deletion

Remove a local capture from Saved. If you also saved it to Apple Photos, manage that copy in the Photos app. To remove cached pose media, choose Clear downloaded artwork in Settings. To remove a favorite, tap its bookmark again.

If you have a Posethetic cloud account and the account tools are available in your build, use Settings → Manage cloud account → Delete cloud account. Deletion may finish after an active job stops. An App Store subscription has its own cancellation controls and transaction handling. For help with a privacy request, contact Posethetic before sharing private information.

This website

The policy and help pages use GitHub Pages. They contain no Posethetic analytics or forms. GitHub handles connection data for the site. Support issues you post on GitHub are public and follow GitHub’s privacy terms. GitHub General Privacy Statement
